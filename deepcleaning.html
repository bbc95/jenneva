Deep Cleaning

A deep cleaning is a more thorough and comprehensive cleaning process than a regular cleaning. It goes beyond superficial tasks and includes cleaning areas that are typically overlooked. The main objectives are:

* Remove accumulated dirt and grime: It focuses on ground-in dirt, dust in hard-to-reach places, and accumulated grease.

* Disinfect and sanitize: It uses specific products to kill germs, bacteria, and viruses, creating a more hygienic environment.

* Improve air quality: By removing dust and allergens, it contributes to cleaner, healthier air.

* Restore shine and freshness: It leaves surfaces sparkling and spaces feeling refreshed. It is performed less frequently than daily or weekly cleaning and is ideal for occasions such as moving, before or after special events, or simply to give a space a "reset."
